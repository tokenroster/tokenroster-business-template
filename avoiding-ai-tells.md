# Avoiding AI Tells

A writing guide for all external copy and anything written in a persona's voice: emails, one-pagers, decks, posts, applications, website copy. The goal is copy that reads like a specific person wrote it for a specific reader, not like a model filled a template.

Internal working notes can be looser, but the hard rules apply everywhere.

---

## Hard rules

- **No em dashes.** Not in documents, not in chat. Use a comma, colon, period, or parentheses, or rewrite the sentence.
- **No invented facts.** Every number comes from `platform-stats.md`. Every claim about a customer, partner, or result must be true and, if named, approved to be public.
- **No placeholder residue.** No `[brackets]`, `TBD`, `{{TOKENS}}`, or "Lorem ipsum" in anything that leaves the repo.

---

## Words and phrases to cut

These read as generated. Replace them with the plain, specific thing.

| Cut | Use instead |
|---|---|
| delve, dive into, unpack | look at, cover, explain |
| leverage, utilize | use |
| seamless, robust, cutting-edge, game-changing, revolutionary | say what it actually does, with a number if you have one |
| empower, unlock, elevate, supercharge | say the concrete outcome |
| in today's fast-paced world, in an ever-evolving landscape | delete; start with the point |
| it's worth noting that, it's important to note | delete; just note it |
| navigate the complexities of | handle, deal with |
| a testament to | shows |
| whether you're X or Y | name the one reader this is for |
| I hope this email finds you well | open with why you're writing |

---

## Patterns to avoid

- **The rule of three by reflex.** Lists of exactly three adjectives or benefits, every time. Use the number of items you actually have.
- **"Not just X, but Y."** And its cousins ("It's not about X, it's about Y"). State Y.
- **Rhetorical-question openers.** "Ever wondered how...?" Start with the claim.
- **Summary closers.** "In conclusion", "Ultimately", "At the end of the day". End on the ask or the last real point.
- **Uniform rhythm.** Every sentence the same length, every paragraph three sentences. Vary it.
- **Hedging stacks.** "This could potentially help to..." Pick one qualifier or none.
- **Emoji and bold as decoration.** Bold is for the one thing a skimming reader must not miss, not every other phrase.
- **Generic praise in outreach.** "I was impressed by your work" with nothing specific. Name the actual thing, or leave it out.

---

## What good looks like

- **One reader.** Write for a named audience with a named problem. If the copy would work unchanged for a different audience, it's too generic.
- **Specific over impressive.** "Cut onboarding from 3 weeks to 4 days" beats "dramatically faster onboarding".
- **Plain verbs.** Use, make, cut, save, ship, sell.
- **Short first sentence.** The reader decides in one line whether to keep reading.
- **A clear ask.** End with one concrete next step the reader can take.

## Before sending

- [ ] No em dashes
- [ ] No word from the cut list
- [ ] Every number traces to `platform-stats.md`
- [ ] Written for one reader, and it would be wrong for any other
- [ ] Read aloud once: anything you wouldn't say to the reader in person gets rewritten
